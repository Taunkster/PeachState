# 05 — Judging Criteria Alignment

## 5.1 Criterion-by-criterion proof map

| Criterion | Where it shows | How the demo proves it |
|-----------|----------------|------------------------|
| **Technical excellence** | Async polling (footer cadence), caching (data_source), OSMnx graph routing (Scene 3), Q10 spoilage kinetics (Scene 3–4) | Footer shows `agent: monitoring 45 fields · 15-min cadence`; Scene 3 names the graph algorithm and Q10 model ("six degrees cooler, cut spoilage by more than half"); risk readouts show model inputs, not just output |
| **Innovation** | Canopy heat risk from 2m API; multi-objective corridor routing; harvest-timing agent | Scene 1: "we fuse 2m heat with humidity and heat index into a canopy risk signal"; Scene 2: harvest agent reasons across risk + exceedance + forecast; Scene 3: "we optimize heat exposure, not miles" |
| **Real-world impact** | Georgia $74B ag economy (Scene 0), USDA alignment (crop thresholds), quantified savings (Scene 4) | Scene 0: "$74B"; Scene 4 KPI cards with units ($, %, t CO₂e); crop thresholds cited from UGA/USDA literature |
| **API usage (4+ endpoints, creative combination)** | `heatmap` (tcm + exceedance), `env_params` (heat_index, humidity, GHI), `heat_intelligence` (PDF) | Every scene names its endpoint; Scene 2 is the creative combo — *heatmap analytic + env_params fused into one harvest-trigger decision*; Scene 4 adds heat_intelligence for buyer reports |
| **Demo quality** | No spinners, pre-cached, snappy | Every transition pre-rendered; fixture mode; fallbacks in `06`; rehearsal checklist in `01` |
| **Climate resilience relevance** | Humid-heat threat to GA agriculture + cold chain | Scene 0 stat chips; Scene 3 spoilage band; crop thresholds table (95°F peaches, 85°F onions) |

## 5.2 Presenter cheat sheet (one line per endpoint)

- `heatmap` → "FortyGuard's hyperlocal temperature snapshots across the orchard"
- `heatmap (exceedance)` → "how long this block has been above its crop threshold"
- `heatmap (persistence)` → "whether the heat is going to stay"
- `env_params` → "heat index and humidity — what the air actually feels like to the fruit"
- `heat_intelligence` → "the boardroom-ready PDF report, generated by the same API"

## 5.3 Track coverage recap

- **Predictive Models** — canopy heat risk scoring + GDD phenology + harvest-window forecast (Scenes 1–2, 4)
- **AI Agents** — harvest-timing agent triggering SMS + packing house coordination + corridor router (Scenes 2–3)
- **Dashboards** — field + cold-chain ops view (Scene 4)
- **Interactive Maps** — risk map + dual-route corridor comparison + community scale-down (Scenes 1, 3, 5)
- **Domains** — Resilient Cities (community gardens, last-mile), Future Buildings/Energy (pre-cooling), Industrial Ops (reefer logistics), Gov/Environment (food security, USDA alignment)

## 5.4 Anti-patterns to avoid on stage

- ❌ Running a terminal / pip install during the demo.
- ❌ Waiting on a spinner (even 1s feels like 10s to judges).
- ❌ Saying "the API might be slow today" — the demo is fixtures-first by design.
- ❌ Reading KPI values verbatim without tying them to a scene the judges just watched.
- ❌ Going over 4:50 — the closing line matters more than the last slide.
- ❌ Mixing °F and °C on screen (all °F, all the time).
